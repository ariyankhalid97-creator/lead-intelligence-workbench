# Local AI curriculum and competency benchmark

Lead Extractor improves local-model consistency through reviewed instruction, retrieval memory and evaluation. It does not silently fine-tune Ollama weights.

## Curriculum

The fixed curriculum covers five practical disciplines:

1. niche-faithful search planning and adjacent-service rejection;
2. literal evidence support, source authority and unknown values;
3. conservative public email-label classification;
4. parent-brand, operating-location, company and person identity boundaries; and
5. qualification and outreach language that does not invent pain or intent.

Synthetic positive and negative examples accompany the rules. Current evidence and deterministic validators remain above curriculum guidance in the authority order.

## Benchmark

Each installed compatible model receives the same JSON-constrained exam. The scorer checks expected phrases and exact categorical answers without asking a second model to judge the first. It records per-task scores, total score and runtime locally. Scores automatically expire when the curriculum version changes.

The benchmark is a regression and routing tool, not a guarantee. It establishes that a model can follow the application's core research boundaries on the included cases. Real output remains subject to evidence validation and user review.
